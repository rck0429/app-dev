# 🎬 My Favorite Series Collection

Here’s a list of some of my all-time favorite series that kept me on the edge of my seat!

---

## 1. **Breaking Bad**

> *"I am not in danger, Skyler. I am the danger."* – Walter White

**Overview**:  
A high school chemistry teacher turned meth kingpin, Walter White partners with his former student Jesse to build an empire.  
An intense story of morality, desperation, and power.

**Main Cast**:
- Walter White (Bryan Cranston)
- Jesse Pinkman (Aaron Paul)
- Skyler White
- Gus Fring

![Breaking Bad]

**Why I Love It**:
- 🔥 Insane character arcs
- 🎥 Cinematic brilliance
- 💥 Constant suspense

---

## 2. **You (Netflix)**

> *"For you, I'd move to the ends of the earth... or just across the street."* – Joe Goldberg

**Overview**:  
A dangerously charming man becomes obsessed with the woman he falls for, leading to manipulations, stalking, and murder—all in the name of love.

**Main Cast**:
- Joe Goldberg (Penn Badgley)
- Love Quinn (Victoria Pedretti)

**Favorite Season**: Season 2  
Joe tries to start fresh in LA but can’t outrun his twisted tendencies.

**Why I Love It**:
- 🧠 Psychological thriller vibes
- 🤯 Unpredictable plot twists
- 👀 Disturbing but fascinating

---

## 3. **Dexter**

> *"I just know there's something dark in me. I hide it. I certainly don't talk about it, but it's there."* – Dexter Morgan

**Overview**:  
Dexter is a forensic blood spatter analyst for the Miami PD, who moonlights as a vigilante serial killer.

**Main Cast**:
- Dexter Morgan (Michael C. Hall)
- Debra Morgan
- Harrison Morgan

**Iconic Moment**:  
When Dexter sets up the plastic room and delivers justice his way.

**Why I Love It**:
- 🔪 Morally complex protagonist
- 🩸 Cool forensic science
- 😈 Dark yet addictive storyline

---

## 4. **How to Sell Drugs Online (Fast)**

> *"If you want to change the world, sell drugs from your bedroom."*

**Overview**:  
A German teenager starts selling ecstasy online to impress his ex-girlfriend—but things spiral out of control fast.

**Main Cast**:
- Moritz Zimmermann
- Lenny Sander
- Lisa Novak

**Standout Feature**:  
Uses quirky narration, fast-paced editing, and real-life tech references to make it super engaging.

**Why I Love It**:
- 💻 Tech meets crime
- 🇩🇪 Underrated European series
- 🧠 Smart, fast, and funny
